Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy covers the Cio Bela Follow & Save page and the Cio Bela feed integration that shows our own TikTok videos on that page.

What the TikTok integration does

We connect only Cio Bela's own TikTok account. Using TikTok's Display API, we read our basic profile information (name and profile photo) and a list of our public videos so they can be played on our page with TikTok's official embedded player. We do not ask visitors to log in to TikTok through our page, and we never access any visitor's TikTok account.

Information about visitors

Our page records anonymous visit information, such as which steps of the page were viewed or tapped and the time of the visit, to understand how the page is used. We do not collect names, emails or TikTok account information from visitors. If you choose to write a review, the text stays on your device and is only copied to your clipboard so you can paste it on Google. Your progress through the page is saved in your own browser.

Third parties

Videos are played through TikTok's embedded player, and Instagram posts are shown through a feed service. Those services have their own privacy policies. Links to Google, TikTok and Instagram open those services directly.

Data retention and deletion

TikTok access credentials for our own account are stored securely and can be revoked by us at any time from TikTok's settings. Anonymous visit records may be kept for business reporting. You can contact us with any privacy question using the details below.
